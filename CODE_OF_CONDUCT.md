# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Tilqos to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, native language, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language. Issues and discussions may be in English, Russian or Kazakh; if you answer in a language the other person did not use, make it easy for them to follow.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticize code and ideas, not people. This includes corrections to Kazakh and Russian text: say what is wrong and what you would write instead.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure about someone's intent.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused and on topic.
- Treat what people type as private. Never paste text someone typed, clipboard contents or window titles into an issue, a pull request or a fixture.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, doxxing, or sexualized language and imagery directed at people.
- Posting other people's private information, or text that someone typed in private.
- Using the project's spaces to share illegal content, or to ask for or promote the use of Tilqos (or anything like it) to spy on other people.
- Spam and unsolicited promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful. Report problems privately through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form (choose "other") or by contacting the maintainers on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
